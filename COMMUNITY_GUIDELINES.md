# Community Guidelines --- 21

**Developer:** Brlek\
**Contact:** brkibb@gmail.com

21 is intended to be a competitive basketball game. Players are expected
to treat other players fairly and respectfully.

## Allowed

-   Competitive play
-   Friendly competition
-   Normal usernames
-   Reporting suspected cheating
-   Reporting technical problems
-   Providing constructive feedback

## Not allowed

-   Harassment or threats
-   Hate or discriminatory content
-   Impersonation
-   Abusive usernames
-   Cheating
-   Bots or unauthorized automation
-   Exploiting bugs
-   Manipulating ranked matches
-   Manipulating leaderboards
-   Fraud or payment abuse
-   Attempts to access another user's account
-   Attempts to disrupt the service

## Reporting

The initial version of 21 does not include text chat.

For problems involving another player, users can contact:

**brkibb@gmail.com**

Future versions that introduce user-generated content or chat should
include appropriate in-app reporting and blocking tools before those
features are released.

## Enforcement

Depending on severity, action may include: - Warning - Removal of
invalid results - Rating correction - Temporary restriction - Temporary
suspension - Permanent account suspension

Google Play's user-generated-content policy requires appropriate
moderation, reporting, and blocking mechanisms when an app contains
user-generated content. Because the initial 21 version has no user chat,
it should not introduce UGC features without implementing the required
