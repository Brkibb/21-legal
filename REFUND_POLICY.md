## Lifetime Premium

21 may offer a **Lifetime Premium** digital purchase, currently planned
at **US\$3**, with the exact price displayed by Google Play.

Premium is intended to provide: - No normal/interstitial
advertisements - No rewarded advertisements required for rerolls - Up to
30 rerolls per day

## Billing

Lifetime Premium is a digital in-app product.

For the Google Play version of 21, digital in-app purchases are handled
through Google Play Billing unless an applicable Google Play policy
exception or alternative billing program applies.

## Refunds

Refunds are subject to the applicable platform's rules and applicable
consumer law.

For purchases made through Google Play, users should use Google's
available purchase/refund process.

The developer does not store full payment-card details.

## Restore purchases

The App will provide a **Restore Purchases** mechanism where supported.

Premium entitlement should be verified against the platform purchase
record rather than trusted solely from local device storage.

## Account deletion

Deleting a 21 account does not automatically constitute a refund.

Before account deletion, the App should clearly explain any effect
deletion may have on access to account-associated Premium entitlement.

## Changes to Premium

The developer may change pricing or benefits for future purchases.

Existing purchases should be handled consistently with the purchase
terms presented to the user and applicable law.
