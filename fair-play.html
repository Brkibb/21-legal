Fair Play Policy --- 21

Developer: Brlek

Purpose

Ranked 21 games depend on trustworthy results. This policy explains

behavior that can compromise competitive integrity.

Prohibited behavior

Players must not: - Modify the client to alter gameplay - Manipulate

network requests - Submit fabricated results - Use bots or unauthorized

automation - Exploit bugs for competitive advantage - Collude to

manipulate ratings - Intentionally manipulate disconnects - Create

multiple accounts to manipulate leaderboards - Abuse rewarded

advertisements or reroll limits - Attempt to bypass Premium or other

restrictions - Interfere with another player's match

Server authority

Important ranked information should be validated server-side,

including: - Player identity - Draft legality - Available sources -

Position restrictions - Match result - Rating changes - Reroll usage -

Premium entitlement - Rewarded-ad rewards

The client should not be trusted to submit an arbitrary final score.

Investigations

Suspicious activity may be reviewed using game records, match history,

timestamps, account information, and technical security data reasonably

necessary for the investigation.

Consequences

Invalid results may be removed and ratings corrected.

Serious or repeated violations may lead to temporary or permanent

suspension.

We may take immediate protective action where necessary to protect the

game or other users.

False reports

Reports should be made honestly. Deliberately abusive or fraudulent

reporting may itself result in account action.
