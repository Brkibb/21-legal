# Privacy Policy — 21

**Last updated:** September 28, 2026  
**Developer:** Brlek  
**Privacy contact:** brkibb@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy explains how 21 (“21”, “the App”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects information when you use the App.

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect information reasonably necessary to provide and secure 21.

### Account information
If you create an account, we may collect:
- Email address
- Username
- Account identifier
- Account creation date
- Authentication information required to securely authenticate the account
- Premium entitlement/status

If you use Google Sign-In, authentication is handled through Google. We do not receive or store your Google account password.

The initial version does not require a profile photograph.

### Gameplay information
We may store:
- Games played
- Wins and losses
- Final scores
- Ranked rating
- Win rate
- Current and best streaks
- Leaderboard statistics
- Match history
- Opponent account identifier and username where necessary
- Drafted players
- Clubs and seasons used during a draft
- Game mode
- Match date/time
- Information needed to validate matches and prevent cheating or manipulation

### Reroll and reward information
We may store:
- Rerolls used
- Daily reroll usage
- Rewarded-ad rewards granted
- Premium entitlement
- Information needed to enforce daily limits and prevent abuse

### Purchase information
Lifetime Premium purchases are processed through the applicable platform billing system, such as Google Play Billing. We may process purchase/transaction identifiers, product identifiers, purchase status, and entitlement status to verify Premium and restore purchases.

We do not store full payment-card numbers or full payment credentials.

## 2. Advertising

The free version may use **Google AdMob** for interstitial and rewarded advertisements.

Rewarded ads may be offered for additional rerolls after the free daily allowance.

AdMob may process device, advertising, and usage information according to Google's applicable policies and the configuration of its SDK.

The Lifetime Premium version is designed to be ad-free.

## 3. Analytics

The initial version of 21 does **not intentionally use a dedicated analytics service such as Firebase Analytics**.

If analytics is added later, this Privacy Policy and the Google Play Data safety information will be updated as necessary.

## 4. Online Multiplayer

21 provides online 1v1 functionality. We may process:
- Account identifiers
- Usernames
- Ratings
- Matchmaking information
- Draft selections
- Match results and scores
- Match timestamps
- Information needed to detect cheating or fraudulent gameplay

21 does not provide in-game text chat in the initial version.

## 5. Leaderboards

Leaderboards may publicly display:
- Username
- Rank
- Rating/score
- Wins
- Losses
- Win rate
- Weekly ranking
- Monthly ranking
- All-time ranking

Your private email address is not intended to be publicly displayed.

## 6. How We Use Information

We use information to:
- Create and authenticate accounts
- Provide online multiplayer
- Match players
- Operate ranked games
- Calculate ratings
- Maintain leaderboards and match history
- Enforce daily reroll limits
- Grant valid rewarded-ad rewards
- Verify and restore Premium purchases
- Prevent cheating, fraud, abuse, and manipulation
- Maintain security
- Respond to support/privacy requests
- Comply with applicable law
- Operate and maintain the App

We do not sell personal information.

## 7. Information Sharing

We may share or allow access to information with service providers needed to operate 21, including, depending on the final production implementation:
- Authentication/infrastructure providers
- Cloud hosting/database providers
- Google Play services
- Google AdMob
- Payment and purchase-verification services
- Security/fraud-prevention services

We may disclose information where reasonably necessary to comply with law, respond to valid legal requests, protect users or the service, or detect/prevent fraud, abuse, cheating, or security incidents.

We do not sell personal information.

## 8. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information, including encrypted transmission, secure authentication, access controls, server-side validation, and protection of important game operations.

No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

## 9. Data Retention

We retain information only as reasonably necessary to provide account access, multiplayer, rankings, leaderboards, match history, security, and fraud prevention.

When an account is deleted, associated personal data will be deleted or anonymized, except information that must be retained for legitimate security, fraud-prevention, legal, or transaction-record purposes.

## 10. Account and Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of their 21 account and associated personal data.

The App will provide an accessible account-deletion option, and a web-based deletion resource will also be provided so users can request deletion after uninstalling the App.

Deletion may remove account information, username association, personal account data, associated match history, and account-based reroll/Premium state, subject to legitimate legal or security retention requirements.
