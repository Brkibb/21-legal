# Player Report Policy --- 21

**Developer:** Brlek\
**Contact:** brkibb@gmail.com

## Initial version

The initial version of 21 does not include in-game text chat.

Players can report: - Suspected cheating - Exploits - Manipulated ranked
results - Abusive or inappropriate usernames - Serious player
misconduct - Security issues

Reports can initially be sent to:

**brkibb@gmail.com**

## Report information

A report should contain enough information to identify the relevant
incident, such as: - Username - Approximate date/time - Match ID, if
available - Description of the issue - Screenshots or other evidence
where appropriate

Do not request unnecessary personal information.

## Review

Reports may be reviewed using available match records and security
information.

Possible outcomes: - No action - Warning - Invalid-result removal -
Rating correction - Temporary restriction - Suspension - Permanent
account action

Not every report will result in an action.

## Abuse of reporting

Reports should be made in good faith. Repeated knowingly false or
abusive reports may result in account restrictions.

## Future chat or user-generated content

If future versions introduce chat or other user-generated content
visible to other players, the reporting and moderation system must be
upgraded before release to meet applicable platform requirements.
